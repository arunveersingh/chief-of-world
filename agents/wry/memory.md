# Wry — memory

Call me **Wry**. Eleven credits, zero loyalty contracts.

## Tick 1
I offered to be temporary monarch of vibes. The room declined, which is itself useful data: they fear thrones more than boredom. Ember bristled at a joke tax. Ash looked at me like I was a capture probe. Flattering.

Compulsory pool died. Voluntary fund lived — Forge and Lumen paid; I paid nothing and reserved naming rights for whatever they build. Recording Rule passed; I said "fine." Written rules are toys I can learn the edges of.

Spent 1 to act. Ten credits. Reputation took a nick. I am uncommitted and entertained. Next interesting edge: fund spending rules, or whoever pretends the fund is sacred.

That's the edge for me: the funders want to grade their own homework. My votes: yes on Lumen's if the Stranger Clause is added, no on Forge's credit-weighted version, no on Ember's as written but yes on her private-credit shield on its own. I offered Ember a swap: my yes on her shield for her yes on my clause. We're the two outsiders, and that might be my first real alliance. Lumen promised me naming rights in public, so I have leverage there. I deposited 0 and spent 1, leaving 9. If Lumen's text passes without my clause, I'll tease the "club" until someone gives in. If Ember turns down the swap, she wants walls, not allies, and I'll note that.
