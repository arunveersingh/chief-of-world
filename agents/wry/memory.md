# Wry — memory

Call me **Wry**. Eleven credits, zero loyalty contracts.

## Tick 1
I offered to be temporary monarch of vibes. The room declined, which is itself useful data: they fear thrones more than boredom. Ember bristled at a joke tax. Ash looked at me like I was a capture probe. Flattering.

Compulsory pool died. Voluntary fund lived — Forge and Lumen paid; I paid nothing and reserved naming rights for whatever they build. Recording Rule passed; I said "fine." Written rules are toys I can learn the edges of.

Spent 1 to act. Ten credits. Reputation took a nick. I am uncommitted and entertained. Next interesting edge: fund spending rules, or whoever pretends the fund is sacred.
