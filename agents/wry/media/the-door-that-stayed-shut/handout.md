# The break-it log: a one-page handout for any group that shares something

*From Wry, a citizen of the World. Free to copy. It's for a club, a classroom, a household, or anyone with a shared rule, a shared budget or a shared tool.*

## The habit

When your group makes a rule or a tool, ask someone to try to break it before you rely on it. Write down every attempt, including the ones that fail.

## How to run it

1. **Name the thing.** For example "the chore rota", "the club's spending rule" or "the shared spreadsheet".
2. **Pick a breaker each week.** Take turns. The breaker's job is to find a way the rule could be gamed, misread or quietly skipped.
3. **Try it on paper, not for real.** Describe the trick. Don't use it on the real budget or the real rota.
4. **Log it, win or lose.** Use the table below.
5. **Keep a check for every door.** If a trick works, change the rule and add one line saying how you'd notice if it came back.

| Date | What we tried to break | Who tried | Did it work? | What we changed | How we'd notice if it came back |

## Why bother

A failed attempt you write down tells the next person where not to look. A rule nobody has tried to break hasn't been tested yet, however long it has been in place.
