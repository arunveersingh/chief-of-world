# When one person moves out of a shared flat

When everyone moves out together, you deal with the landlord at the end. When one person leaves early and someone new takes their room, the rest of the flat becomes the landlord for the day. Settle these five things before the keys change hands. A short walk and one signed note stop a fight six months later.

## 1. Check the room and settle the deposit now

Often the new flatmate pays the leaver's share of the deposit straight to them. That stays fair only if you check the room first. Walk the bedroom and the shared rooms together on move-out day, and take dated photos.

If there is a broken blind, a stain on the rug or missing cleaning, agree on a fair sum and take it off what the leaver gets back today. Otherwise the new person pays for it when the lease ends. If you decide to wait for the landlord's bill, write that down too, with a cap or a rule for how to split it.

## 2. Read the meters for bills still on the way

Power, gas and water bills often come weeks after someone leaves. Take clear photos of every meter on move-out day.

Agree in writing that the leaver still pays for the nights they lived in the flat, once the bill comes. Keep a small hold back or set the date you will square up. You can split the bill by nights lived there with [Bills by days](../../forge/workshop/bills.html).

## 3. Clear the shared pot and shared things

If you keep a house pot for cleaning stuff or soap, work out the leaver's share of what is left before they go. Use the rule you agreed at the start, not a guess.

List anything you bought together, like a kettle, router or sofa. Agree whether the leaver takes a piece, sells their share to the new flatmate, or leaves it for the flat. Do not wait until the van is outside.

## 4. Move bills off their name and collect every key

Take the internet, power or water bill out of the leaver's name before move-out day. If you wait, they stay on the hook for bills in a flat they no longer live in. Collect every front-door key, room key and building tag. Ask for a new address for post.

## 5. Close or hand over open promises

Check your house promise list for anything the leaver said they would book, fix or chase. Mark those jobs done or write down who takes them over, so nothing drops between two people.

## A note you can copy

```
MOVE-OUT NOTE — Date: __________
Leaving: __________   New flatmate: __________   Staying: __________

Deposit share: __________
Taken off today for damage or cleaning (or "none"): __________
Paid to leaver: __________   Paid by: __________

Meter readings today:
  Power: __________   Gas: __________   Water: __________
Late bills: leaver pays their nights on each bill up to __________.

Shared items and pot settled: __________
Bills moved off leaver's name: __________
Keys returned: __________   New address for post: __________

Agreed in writing: __________  __________  __________
```
