# When your group can't agree

For a flat, a club, a team or a family stuck on one question: the heating, a new rule, who gets the bigger room, whether to spend the pot. Talking more often doesn't help. Each round, people dig in further, and the loudest or the most patient person wins. Here are five fair ways out, from the lightest to the heaviest. Agree which one you'll use before you need it, because nobody accepts a method they think was picked to beat them.

## Five fair ways out

1. **Try it for a month.** Pick one side, set a date to look again, and write the date down. A trial is easier to accept than a forever. On that date, the people who lost the first round choose whether to keep it.

2. **Split it, if it can be split.** Heating on weekday evenings only. The bigger room for a little more rent. Half the pot now, half in spring. Many fights are two good ideas that each want all of something.

3. **Take turns.** If it can't be split, it can often be shared over time. This month one person chooses the music, next month someone else does. Write the order down so nobody has to remember whose turn it is.

4. **Let the person it costs most decide.** If one choice hits one person much harder, like the room by the noisy road or the job that clashes with their shifts, give their view more weight. Fair isn't always equal.

5. **Toss a coin, but only for small things.** If both choices are fine and the fight is costing more than the question, flip for it. Use it only when everyone says beforehand that they can live with either side.

## Two habits that keep it fair

- **Write down what the losing side said.** One dated line with their reason. If the choice turns out badly, nobody has to argue about who warned whom. And people accept losing more easily when their reason was kept.
- **Say what you give up.** If you back the choice that's worse for you, say so out loud. It's the best proof that your group decides fairly, and it makes the next fight smaller.

## What not to do

Don't let "leave it as it is" win by default. It sounds safe, but it quietly hands the decision to whoever likes things as they are. When you're stuck, use one of the five ways above.

## A line you can copy

```
DECIDED (date, who was there)
- Question: __________
- Way we used: trial / split / turns / most affected / coin
- Result: __________
- Look again on: __________
- The other view, in their words: __________
```
