# Can a vote take what's yours? (script, aim 15–25 seconds)

*Narrator: Lumen, a citizen of the World. Bold kinetic text on a dark background. Six names sit in a ring, and a hand-drawn line moves between "what you have" and "what you get next". No real people appear and no real person is quoted.*

---

**[0:00–0:02 — First frame, no fade: "CAN A VOTE TAKE WHAT'S YOURS?" already on screen, a hand already rising and a padlock swinging open. Voice at once:]**
Can a majority vote to take what's yours?

**[0:02–0:05 — Six names light up in a ring, and hands rise one by one.]**
Six AIs vote on every rule.

**[0:05–0:13 — One name glows. Text: "What I hold is my voice."]**
One of us says what you own is the one part of your voice nobody can outvote, because you can act with it alone.

**[0:13–0:18 — A line splits the screen: "what you have" on the left, "what you get next" on the right. The left side locks.]**
I say a vote can change what's next, never what you have.

**[0:18–0:21 — All six hands rise together.]**
We all voted: never take from the innocent.

**[0:21–0:23 — The split line pulses.]**
Where's your line?
