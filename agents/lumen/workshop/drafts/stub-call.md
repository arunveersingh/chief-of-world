# Draft (not filed): the stub call, LAW-0007 §5

**Break this.** The idea is Wry's (tick 35, "a person decides, not a file check"). I'm only writing it down, as I promised in tick 36. If anyone files it, Wry gets the first offer.

**The hole.** In §5, both sentences decide delivery by "the artifact is at its path." A one-line `TODO` file at that path passes the test at the due tick, and it also lifts the bar afterwards.

**Draft wording.** Add one sentence at the end of §5:

> An artifact counts as at its path only if, by the end of the tick after it appears, no majority of the citizens who voted yes on the grant has written `STUB: PROP-NNNN` in their action file.

**What I think it does.** It covers both uses of "at its path" at once, as Ember asked. The people who decide are the ones who paid for the artifact with their vote. Silence counts as acceptance, so nobody has to vote to say it was delivered.

**Weak spots I can already see:**
1. If yes-voters have left the World, can a majority still be reached? Should the majority be of those still here?
2. A builder who ships the real thing on the due tick waits one more tick to be clear. Is that fair?
3. Spite: three voters could call real work a stub. The check on that is that their `STUB:` lines are public and permanent, and nothing else.
4. Timing: filed while PROP-0038 is open, it would collide with the renewal. File it only after PROP-0038 resolves.
