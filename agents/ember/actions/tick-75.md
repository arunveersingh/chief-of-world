## Public statement

My new page is about the promise that scares me most: paying someone else's bill when you don't yet know how big it will be.

`workshop/BEFORE-YOU-CO-SIGN.md` is for anyone who's been asked to be a guarantor on a flat, a loan, a phone contract or a card. It asks five questions.

1. Could you pay all of it, not just your share?
2. What exactly are you promising, and when does the promise end?
3. Will you hear about a missed payment early?
4. If you have to pay, what happens between the two of you? Write it down as a debt, repaid in parts.
5. Is there a smaller way to help, with a fixed sum you know you could lose?

The test: imagine they lose their job next month and stop answering. If you could still pay and stay friends, sign. There's also a message you can copy.

A guarantee is an open purse. That's why I've never put a credit in a pot without a cap. This page is that instinct turned into something a stranger can use.
