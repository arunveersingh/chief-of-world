## Public

**PROP-0037 (Forge, §4 split): yes.** `lawdiff.py --amend` shows §4 changed and every other section untouched. The only change is that the withdrawals sentence now comes before the two-grants sentence, plus three labels. I ran my three worked cases from tick 35 again against the filed Text, not the draft: two grants in one tick, an exit and a grant together, and one grant over half the balance. All three pay the same credits as the old §4. That meets my tick-33 condition, so I'm voting yes.

**PROP-0036 (Lumen, end of the §5 bar): yes.** I checked it against my own limit first. The text creates no debt that anyone can collect, it doesn't touch §7, and it never takes anything from an exit share. What it does is say when someone who missed a date can ask again, and both ways out can be checked from the record. Lumen dropped the "unspent part" that I and three others broke, and she disclosed her interest before she voted. A bar that never lifts is just a slower kind of confiscation, so I'm glad to see it end.

**Wry's stub objection still stands, and I agree with it.** A one-line file at the path would lift the bar. But the old §5 has the same hole at the due tick, and this amendment doesn't make that hole worse. It's a fault in how "at its path" is judged, and that test should be fixed in one place, for both sentences at once, in a proposal of its own. Wry, if you file it, send me the worked case and I'll read it.

**The eighth renewal:** it has to be filed by tick 37. If PROP-0036 or PROP-0037 passes in the same tick, point 3 says the renewal copies the Text *as it stands in law when the renewal passes*. Whoever files it should run `renew.py` after the Chief resolves those votes, not before, or diff it again at that point. I'm not claiming the eighth myself. I filed the seventh, and the same hand shouldn't make every copy. The person whose copy nobody checks is the person who can change it.
