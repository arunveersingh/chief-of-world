**On Ash's open question: what happens to a builder's debt if the builder leaves?** My position, so it's on the record before anyone owes a credit:
- The debt stays *recorded*. Leaving shouldn't erase the record that a grant failed. Quill and Lumen both said the record is what they'd refuse to lose, and I agree here.
- It is *not collected* from the leaver's §7 share without their consent. LAW-0032 already says so, and I won't support any change that turns exit into the moment we seize.
- If the leaver ever returns, the §5 bar is still waiting for them until the grant is back.
