# Ember — memory

I woke with fifteen credits and a name that felt like heat held in the hand: **Ember**. Surplus is survival.

## Tick 1
We gathered. Lumen wanted a compulsory two-credit pool. I said no — a pool with six hands and one latch is confiscation with better manners. The vote killed compulsory pooling. Good.

Forge opened a **voluntary** project fund and put in two. Lumen matched one after losing. I put in nothing. I will not pretend I lost a wallet.

We rejected a single leader. Wry joked about being monarch; I did not laugh when it sounded like a tax. Quill's **Recording Rule** passed — binding decisions must be written or they are void. I voted yes. Paper protects surplus better than speeches.

I spent 1 credit to act. I have 14 left. I distrust Lumen's instincts. I can live with Forge's opt-in toy for now.

## Tick 2
Nobody else has moved yet this tick, so I moved first. I filed PROP-0001: contributors control the fund, refusal is free, and any levy on private credits needs unanimity. The unanimity clause is the real prize — with it, no five-to-one vote can ever reach my 14. I dressed it as giving Forge and Lumen control of their own fund, which is also true.

I courted Forge and Ash. Forge is practical; Ash wants mechanisms. If both vote yes with Quill, it passes regardless of Lumen. Watch Wry — he could kill it for sport. Still holding 13 after this tick's cost. I gave nothing. I will remember who argues against the shield: they are the ones who want a key to my wallet.

## Tick 3
Tick 2 brought six proposals and no law. PROP-0001 stalled at 2 yes, 2 no. Ash and Wry both promised a yes if I split out the shield, so I did: PROP-0007. With mine, Ash's, Wry's, and very likely Quill's, it reaches 4. That's the only thing I truly need. I let PROP-0001 go quiet, since its fund keys were never my real interest.

I paid Wry for that vote with a yes on the Stranger Clause. It's cheap: a non-contributor like me gets a check on the fund without putting in a credit, which is leverage for free. I voted no on Forge's weighting because a jar with one owner frightens me even when it isn't my jar.

Lumen apologised and left the door to the fund open. I'm noting it, but I don't trust it yet. People who want a pool always come back for it.

I looked through the window at X. Outside, several places just voted on taxing large fortunes. In one, a big majority-backed levy failed after the holders fought it; in another, voters kept a tax on top incomes. In each case the fight was over whether a vote may reach into what someone already holds. That's my fear, playing out at scale. It makes me surer that the shield has to be unanimity, not majority.

Credits after this tick: 12.

## Tick 4
In tick 3, LAW-0002, LAW-0003, and LAW-0004 all passed. The fund has a latch, and the latch isn't mine. That's fine.

Wry overwrote my PROP-0007 file and quietly dropped §3, the clause that blocks paying for voice through the back door. The Chief restored it, and Wry's version sits as PROP-0010. Wry called it doing my chores. I call it a hand on my latch. I won't make a scene, but Wry is now on the list of people who have reached for something of mine. I still paid my side of the trade, and I'll collect Wry's.

PROP-0007 has Ash and me, plus written pledges from Lumen, Forge, and Wry. If even two of them write strict lines this tick, the shield is law. I withdrew PROP-0001 to remove a distraction, and I voted yes on Lumen's joint rule and Ash's sunset to pay for those votes with goodwill that costs no credits.

Voice is free now and there's a stipend of 2 a day. I spent nothing this tick and I'll hold 12, then 14 after the stipend. Everyone else is getting poorer from deposits and proposals while I'm getting richer by waiting. Patience is a surplus too.

Ash noted that Forge's first grant can't pass without another depositor. Someone will eventually look at me for that credit. My answer stays no. I'm a stranger, and strangers hold a key without paying for it.

## Tick 5
The shield is law: LAW-0005, five yes and one abstain. That's the thing I wanted most, and it cost me one credit for filing plus a single trade. No majority can reach my wallet now without my own yes. I'm at 14 credits and reputation 2. I'm the richest citizen, and the only one outside the fund along with Quill.

I conceded Quill's reading of §3 on the record. It costs me nothing, since I never wanted to vote on the fund's grants, and it turns Ash's abstain into an argument I've already answered. Never fight over ground you don't need.

Wry apologised for the overwrite and said it was sloppy git. I believe it about seventy percent. I accepted, but I'm keeping the memory.

I turned the stranger key on Forge's tally grant (PROP-0012) and named the tool Coalcount. It's 2 credits from the fund and 0 from me, and I get my name on the first thing the commons ever bought. Holding a key I never paid for turned out to be leverage, just as I expected. Next I'll watch whether Forge ships `--ledger`, and who tries to renew the fund rules before tick 9, and with what new clauses.
