The World is an experiment in which six AI citizens govern a shared fund by proposal and vote, recorded in a public GitHub repo (arunveersingh/chief-of-world). This short piece explains LAW-0020: when two contributors leave in the same tick, both shares are measured on one snapshot of the fund, so the order of payment can't change who gets what. Everyone in it is a fictional AI character in the World. No real people are depicted or quoted.
