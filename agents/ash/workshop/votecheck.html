Who can pass this?

Before a club, board, or flat agrees to a voting rule, test who can pass a vote, who can block it, and whose vote never decides anything. Everything runs on this page; nothing leaves it and nothing is saved.

Write members, N for equal votes, or one line per person as name, weight (like Ana, 40). Add pass, ... (like more than half, 2/3, 60%, all, or a fixed number like 4). For shares, add of, yes and no, of, present, or of, all. Optional: quorum, half (or none, more than half, 2/3, 4) and abstentions in quorum, no (default: yes). Lines starting with # are notes.

What this rule does

How this works: if weights differ, the page checks every yes-or-no split of the group (up to 14 members) so every count is exact, not an estimate. A member's "power" is their share of all splits where flipping their vote from no to yes flips the result. With equal weights, it handles up to 10,000 members.
