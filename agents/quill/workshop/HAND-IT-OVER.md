# Hand it over: how to pass on a job so the group doesn't forget

For a club treasurer, a team lead, a school committee, the person who books the family holiday. Anyone may copy this page.

When someone steps down from a job, the group often loses what only they knew. Where is the money? What did they promise? Why is the rule the way it is? Six months later nobody remembers, and old fights start again. One hour and one page can stop that.

## Five things to hand over

1. **Money and accounts.** Write down every balance, with the date you read it. List each account, login and card that goes with the job. Move them into the new person's name the same week. Never send a password in a group chat; change it after the handover instead.
2. **Open promises.** List everything you said you would do and haven't done yet: a booking, a refund, a reply. For each one, write who takes it over. If nobody does, say so out loud and cross it off together.
3. **Decisions and their reasons.** Point to the group's decision log, or write the three or four choices people will ask about. Say what was decided, when, and why. A rule without a reason is the first one people try to undo.
4. **Dates that are coming.** Renewals, fees, deadlines, the next meeting. Put them in the new person's calendar before you leave.
5. **What went wrong.** Name the mistakes you made and how you fixed them. This is the part people skip, and it saves the new person the most time.

## Do it in front of someone

Hand over with a third person present, or post the page where the whole group can see it. Both of you sign and date it. Then the old holder can't be blamed for what they passed on, and the new holder can't be blamed for what they were never told.

## A note you can copy

```
HANDOVER — Job: __________   Date: __________
From: __________   To: __________   Witness: __________
Balances (date read): ______________________________
Accounts moved: ____________________________________
Open promises, and who has them: ___________________
Decisions to keep, with reasons: ___________________
Dates coming up: ___________________________________
Mistakes and fixes: ________________________________
Signed: __________   __________
```
