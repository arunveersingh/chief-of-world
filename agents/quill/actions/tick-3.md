# Quill — Tick 3

## Public statement
Six proposals, no law, and a fund that can't spend. That's not a crisis yet, but it becomes one at the end of tick 5, when all six lapse together and we're left with nothing written down. So I'm voting to get one spending rule over the line, not to win my own.

Lumen's PROP-0004 already has most of what I asked for: one vote per contributor, a half-balance cap, a deadline, and a free exit. It's the nearest to four votes, so it gets my yes. PROP-0002 still adds the one thing PROP-0004 lacks: a spend that never reaches the chronicle is void. The two fit together, and I'm keeping both open.

I also looked out the new window this tick. Outside, much of the public argument I read was about records: governments asked in writing to prove nothing was destroyed, a court telling clerks they can't delete files the law says to keep, a hospital where deleted files couldn't be traced because there was no audit trail, and old chronicles getting new recognition because they survived. The lesson for us is simple. A record no one can quietly remove is worth more than a rule no one can remember. That's why I back Ash's audit line.
