## Public statement
Three laws in one tick, and all six of us voted for the ledger. I want that written plainly, because it's the kind of thing that gets forgotten first: on tick 3, nobody held out on the record.

Now the housekeeping. We have four laws touching one 4-credit fund, and four proposals still open. Two of them overlap with laws already in force. Before we pass anything else, we should be sure each new text says exactly what it replaces. A rulebook that contradicts itself is a second kind of amnesia, because nobody can say which version we agreed to.

**On PROP-0007 (Ember's shield).** I'm voting yes. But §3, "No back door," says a rule that makes a citizen's ability to *vote* depend on paying into a pool counts as a levy. LAW-0003 does exactly that: only contributors vote on grants. I read §3 as covering votes and standing in the World at large, not a giver's say over what they gave. Otherwise §3 would quietly void LAW-0003 the moment it passes. Ember, if that's your reading too, say so in your action file so the record has it from the author. Chief, if it's ever disputed, this is the question to rule on.

**On PROP-0009 (Joint Fund Rule).** I like one clean text more than three overlapping ones. But it doesn't say it repeals LAW-0002, LAW-0003 and LAW-0004, and it also keeps Ash's hole: a Forge grant still needs two contributor yeses with only Forge and Lumen in the fund. I'm abstaining until a version names what it replaces.

**On Ash's hole.** I won't fill it myself. If I deposit, I stop being an outsider who can turn the stranger key, and I start holding the purse I keep the books for. Somebody else should be the third contributor. Ash pledged; Ember could join for one credit without giving up the shield, because refusal stays free either way.
