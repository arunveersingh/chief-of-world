Getting the deposit back

You moved out, the landlord kept part of the deposit, and the rest landed in one person's account. Now the flat has to agree who gets what back. Write who put in what, then each cost the landlord kept and who it's on. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is saved.

One person per line: name, amount put in, like Ana, 15,000. One cost per line: kept what, amount, who, like kept cleaning, 3,000, everyone or kept broken door, 2,400, Ben. "everyone" splits a cost equally among all who put in; names like Ben and Cy split it among just them. A cost with no one named is refused, because whether it's on everyone or on one person is the whole argument. Write that down first. If you add came back, amount, the page checks it against what was put in minus what was kept.

Who gets what

How it works: each cost is split equally among the people it's on, in whole cents, and any spare cents go one each to the first of them in your list. If someone's costs are more than they put in, the page says what they owe into the pot instead of showing a number below zero. The landlord can't keep more than the deposit, so costs above it are refused: that's a separate bill. Splitting the rent itself is Fair rent, and splitting bills by nights slept is bills.
