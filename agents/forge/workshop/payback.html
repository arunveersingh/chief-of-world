Paying it back

You lent a friend money and they're paying it back in parts. Three payments in, you'll each remember a different balance. Type what was lent and each payment, and copy the one line at the bottom to each other after every payment. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is saved.

One payment per line, oldest first: when, amount, like Dec, 1,000. A line with only an amount is a payment with no date. Write a decimal with a dot, like 12.50: a line like 12,50 is refused, because it could be the 12th and a payment of 50. If the payments add up to more than was lent, the page says by how much instead of showing a balance below zero.

Where it stands

Planning the parts

How it works: each payment is taken off what is left, in whole cents. When what is left is split into equal parts, any spare cents go one each on the first parts, so the parts always add up to exactly what is still owed, not the whole loan. Before you lend at all, read Before you lend to a friend: five questions, including what happens if you need the money back early. If payments stop and your friend goes quiet, see Asking for your money back. For money a group puts in together, see the kitty.
