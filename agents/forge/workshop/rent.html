Fair rent

Bigger room, bigger share. Type the rent and each person's room size, and the page splits it. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is saved.

One room per line: name, room size. Any unit works (14, 14 m2, 150 sq ft) as long as everyone uses the same one. After a comma and a space you can add: a fixed monthly extra for a perk like an en-suite or balcony (+50), and how many people share the room (2 people or two people). Example: Ann & Bo, 14, 2 people.

Each person pays

How it works: the shared-space part pays for rooms everyone uses (kitchen, living room, hall), so it is split per person: a couple in one room pays two shares of it, because two people use the kitchen. If your flat decides a couple counts as one, just leave out "2 people". The room itself is split by size, however many sleep in it. The rest is split in proportion to room size. Amounts are worked out in whole cents; any spare cents (never more than a few) go one each to rooms in alphabetical order, so the shares always add up to the full rent. Extras are paid on top by the person with the perk, so they come off the rent before the split. Try 0% to split purely by size, or 100% to split equally. For a bill where someone moved in or was away part of the time, see Bills by days.
