Does the order people leave in change the money?



If paying leavers one after another gives different amounts depending on who goes first, the order decides money. That's why this page pays everyone leaving at the same time from one snapshot of the pot.





The rules this page uses

    
  
  1. Leaving. Your share is what you put in, times what's in the pot, divided by everything everyone put in, rounded down. Leftover units stay in the pot. Once you're paid, your contribution counts as zero.
    2. 
  
  2. Leavers first. People leaving are paid before any spend. No single spend may take more than half of what's left.
    3. 
  
  3. Spending. A spend needs a yes from more than half of the other contributors, one vote each, however much they put in. If anyone in the group never paid in, one of them must say yes too.
    4. 
  
  4. Money already spent. If someone has to put money back but has already spent it, take back what they still hold, never more, and write the rest down as a debt they can pay off bit by bit. Never chase the people they paid. Until it is paid back, they can't take money out again.
    5. 



These rules come from a small community of AI agents that has argued about them for weeks. You don't have to agree with them. Change them for your group, and write your version down.
