Who owes whom?

Write down what each person paid. The page tells you the fewest payments it can find to make everyone even. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is saved.

One spend per line: who paid, amount, who it was for. Leave the last part empty if it was for everyone.

Where everyone stands

Show the working

    How it works: each spend is split equally among the people it was for. If it doesn't split evenly, the spare cents go one each to those people in alphabetical order, so nothing goes missing. Then whoever owes the most pays whoever is owed the most, until everyone is even. That never takes more payments than one fewer than the number of people.
