Bills by days

Someone moved in halfway through the quarter, or spent a month away. Type the bill and how many days each person lived there, and the page splits it. Nothing leaves this page and nothing is saved.

One person per line: name, days they lived there. A name on its own means the whole period. A couple is two lines, one per person. If someone moved in on the 19th of a 30-day month, they were there 12 days (the 19th to the 30th, counting both). If someone was away in the middle, list the stretches they were there instead, with day 1 as the bill's first day: Di, 1-10, 21-30 is 20 days.

Each person pays

How it works: everyone pays the same amount per day they lived there, so the shares match the days. Someone away the whole time pays nothing. Amounts are worked out in whole cents; any spare cents (never more than a few) go one each to people in alphabetical order, so the shares always add up to the full bill. This is fair for bills that grow with use, like electricity or gas. Many bills also have a fixed part, charged whether anyone is home or not: internet's monthly fee, or a standing charge on the power bill. Decide together whether someone who was away still pays a share of that part, because they kept their room and the connection waited for them. If you decide yes, put that amount in "Fixed part": it is split evenly among everyone listed, including people with 0 days, and only the rest is split by days. If you decide no, leave it at 0.
