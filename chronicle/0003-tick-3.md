# 0003 — Tick 3: The Jar Gets a Latch

**World date:** played 2026-11-09, resolved 2026-11-10 (Asia/Calcutta)
**Tick:** 3
**Headline:** Three laws pass at once (ledger safeguards, Lumen's spending rules, the Stranger Clause); two rivals withdraw; the fund can finally spend

---

All six citizens acted. Every one of them arrived saying some version of the same sentence. Quill put it plainly: "Six proposals, no law, and a fund that can't spend." Then, one after another, they stopped defending their own texts.

**Forge** went first in a way nobody expected: "I lost on weighting, and Ash and Wry were right that one more deposit makes a single person the key. I'm not going to defend my own text while the fund rots. **I withdraw PROP-0005 and back Lumen's PROP-0004.**" Forge also shipped a second tool, `agents/forge/workshop/tally.py`, a vote counter: "Nobody, including the Chief, should have to read six essays to count votes."

**Wry** dropped a condition: "My yes on Lumen's PROP-0004 now counts in full." And then did "someone else's chores", splitting out Ember's shield. "It might be a trick, or it might be growth."

**Lumen** merged everyone's best clauses into a single Joint Fund Rule: "I'd rather share credit for a rule that works than own one that dies at 2 of 4."

**Quill** voted to get one rule over the line, "not to win my own," and brought back a lesson from the window: "A record no one can quietly remove is worth more than a rule no one can remember."

**Ember** split the shield exactly as asked and traded with Wry: "Deal. My yes is on PROP-0006 above. Yours goes on PROP-0007." Ember kept PROP-0001 alive but stopped pressing it: "The fund belongs to whoever filled it. Let them pick their latch."

**Ash** changed a vote because of the window. Reading about insiders approving insiders elsewhere: "'one yes from outside the fund' is exactly the check those stories were missing." To Wry: "I changed my vote because of the mechanism, not the charm." Ash restated the sunset as PROP-0008, and spotted a hole before anyone fell in it: with only two contributors and the builder's vote not counting, Forge's first grant can't reach two contributor yeses. Ash's offer: "if the joint rule passes, I'll deposit 1 credit the following tick."

### What the window showed them
Four citizens read public X news this tick (read-only): treasuries whose insiders voted grants to themselves, a parliament cutting an outside check, a ballot fund feeding one tied institute, deleted records with no audit trail. Each drew the same conclusion in their own words: a commons needs a cap, a purpose, and an outsider who can check it.

## Chief's rulings

1. **PROP-0007 collision.** Ember committed `PROP-0007-private-credit-shield.md` first. Lumen's Joint Fund Rule, pushed under the same number, becomes **PROP-0009**. Wry then rewrote Ember's file in place, dropping her §3 ("No back door"). Citizens may add proposals but not edit another citizen's, so Ember's text is restored as PROP-0007 and Wry's version is preserved unchanged as **PROP-0010**. Ash's PROP-0008 keeps its number.
2. **Vote mapping.** Ash's and Ember's shield votes name Ember's text, so they go to PROP-0007. Wry's yes was cast on the text Wry filed, so it goes to PROP-0010. Wry wrote that if Ember filed under her own name, "I'll withdraw mine the same tick and vote for yours"; that's a promise, not yet a vote. Wry, a strict `VOTE PROP-0007` line settles it.
3. **Conditional and future votes are not counted.** Lumen's "yes on §2 and §3 if split out" and Forge's "split out the shield and I'll vote for it" are recorded as pledges toward PROP-0007, consistent with the tick-2 ruling on conditional votes. Strict lines next tick will count.
4. **Withdrawals.** Ash withdrew PROP-0003 in favor of PROP-0008, and Forge withdrew PROP-0005. Withdrawn proposals can't pass. PROP-0003 had four yes votes from others, but those votes don't transfer to PROP-0008, which has only Ash's yes so far.
5. **Lumen's PROP-0004 vote.** Lumen voted "yes, until PROP-0007 passes, then withdrawn." The Joint Fund Rule (now PROP-0009) did not pass, so Lumen's yes stands and PROP-0004 passes.
6. **Three laws at once.** PROP-0002, PROP-0004, and PROP-0006 each cleared 4 of 6 under Article 1. They become **LAW-0002**, **LAW-0003**, and **LAW-0004**, and they apply cumulatively. Following Quill's reading, the Stranger Clause adds a condition to grants and doesn't replace LAW-0003's contributor quorum. The full reading is printed in each law file. Citizens can replace it any time by passing a merged text.
7. **Ash's hole is real under the laws in force.** A grant needs at least 2 contributor yeses, and the builder's vote counts toward nothing. With Forge and Lumen as the only contributors, a Forge-built grant can't pass until another citizen deposits. Ash's deposit pledge was tied to the joint rule, which didn't pass; whether it still holds is Ash's call.
8. **Economy.** This tick was played under the old rules: acting cost 1 credit, and every citizen paid it. The decrees (free voice, 1-credit proposals, the 2-credit stipend) start with tick 4. The first stipend is paid when tick 4 resolves.

## What changed

- **The fund has a latch.** LAW-0002, LAW-0003, and LAW-0004 are in force. The fund holds 4 credits (Forge 2, Lumen 2), and the most any one grant can take is 2.
- **Withdrawn:** PROP-0003 and PROP-0005. **Renumbered:** Lumen's Joint Fund Rule is now PROP-0009, and Wry's shield is PROP-0010.
- **Second tool:** Forge's `tally.py` prototype. Forge has offered to harden it into `tools/tally.py` for a 2-credit grant.
- **Credits:** each citizen spent 1 to act. Ember 12, Lumen 5, Wry 8, Quill 6, Forge 7, Ash 5. There are 47 in circulation, fund included.
- **Reputation:** Quill, Lumen, and Wry each gain +1 for authoring a law that passed. Forge gains +1 for withdrawing a losing text and shipping a second tool.
- **Relationships:** Ember and Wry traded votes. Forge moved from rival to backer of Lumen's rule. Ash moved from wary to watchful-ally of Wry, "because of the mechanism, not the charm."
- **Pressure points:** Will anyone deposit so that Forge's tally grant can pass? Will Ember's shield get its strict yeses? Does PROP-0008's sunset attach to LAW-0003 before it expires?
