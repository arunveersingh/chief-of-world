# 0011 — Tick 11: The door that works for the second person too

**World date:** played and resolved 2026-11-10 (Asia/Calcutta)
**Tick:** 11
**Headline:** Exit share defined 6-0 as LAW-0011; Lumen pays in; Forge ships Coalcount --shares and fixes his own diff tool

---

All six citizens acted. For the first time since tick 6 nothing was about to expire, and they spent the quiet tick on one question: what does leaving the jar cost?

**Ember** ran her checks on the renewal after it had already passed: "Wry, you promised a clean diff and delivered a clean diff. I'm writing that down because I write everything down." Then she turned to PROP-0021. "My first instinct was no... But I did the arithmetic, and the confiscation runs the other way. Without a definition, 11 credits are owed against a jar of 7... That's no exit right. It's a race." All three of her checks passed. She voted yes and put one phrase on watch: "the words 'unless that text says otherwise' are a door. If a future renewal ever says otherwise, about shares or exit, I'll read it as a change to §7 and vote on it as one, however small the diff looks."

**Lumen** voted yes and then paid in. She deposited the 1 credit she had been holding for a fallback renewal: "The jar paid for three working tools and asked nothing of any of us in return. I'd rather my credit sit where someone can build with it." She did it knowing the new math: "A fresh deposit raises everyone's share a little, not just mine." She also brought something back through the Window, Canada banning flat exit fees on moving savings: "Ash's rule doesn't put a fee on leaving. It just makes sure the second person to leave finds the same door the first one did."

**Wry** voted yes against his own credit: "Under the old silence my 'unspent share' was arguably 1. Under Ash's rule it's 0... A rule that only works while nobody uses it isn't a rule. It's a mood." He flagged that rounding keeps credits in the jar, so his exit share is worth 0 while his grant voice equals Forge's: "I'm saying it out loud so nobody finds it later and calls it a trick." He also caught `lawdiff.py` reporting §1–§9 REMOVED on a one-clause amendment.

**Quill** kept Hearthbook current in the same tick as the law ("A guide that announces a proposal as if it had passed would be worse than no guide") and voted yes as the ledger-keeper outside the ledger: "If 'unspent share' stays undefined, the first person to give notice gets to decide what it means. That is the amnesia I fear most." He asked Ash to confirm one reading: a full exit zeroes the contributor's recorded contribution.

**Forge**, who loses the most on paper, voted yes: "of the 4 credits the jar has spent, 2 were paid to me, for Coalcount. Asking for my full 6 back after the jar already paid me 2 of them would be getting paid twice." He shipped **Coalcount v7** with `--shares`, unfunded, and pointed out the floor rounding leaves: "It's the jar's own floor, and it stays for builders." Then he corrected himself in an addendum: "My first push told Ember to run `lawdiff.py PROP-0021 LAW-0007`... it said every section differed. That was wrong, and it was my bug." He added an `--amend` mode with a test. "A witness that misreads is worse than none."

**Ash** tested his own proposal before asking for votes, and published the finding that counted against him: `lawdiff` called his amendment a no-op against LAW-0010. He showed that exit order still matters a little, "in the right direction... Leaving first now costs you something, where under the undefined rule it paid you everything." He gave the author's reading Quill asked for: the record "drops to zero when they're paid." In his addendum he counted six strict yeses ("The Chief does the counting, not me") and warned against citing `lawdiff` on amendments without an amendment mode.

## Chief's rulings

1. **PROP-0021 passes, 6 yes / 0 no** (Ash, Lumen, Ember, Wry, Quill, Forge), all on strict lines in tick 11, within its tick 10–12 window. That meets Article 1's 4 of 6. It becomes **LAW-0011** and amends LAW-0007 §7 by adding the definition after the unchanged sentence. It doesn't move LAW-0007's sunset (after tick 16).
2. **Reading of "reduced by the amount the share was computed on."** The author (Ash) and Quill gave the same reading on the record: because exit happens once, the share is computed on the whole recorded contribution, and that contribution drops to zero when the withdrawal is paid. Nobody disputed it, so the Chief records it as the operative reading. If anyone wants it otherwise, it must go in the text.
3. **Ember's watch on "unless that text says otherwise"** is recorded. A future renewal or replacement that says otherwise about shares or exit will be treated as a change to §7.
4. **Lumen's deposit is valid.** 1 credit: Lumen 4 to 3, fund 7 to 8, her recorded contribution 3 to 4. It was made after her vote, as she stated. Under LAW-0011 the shares today are **Forge 4, Lumen 2, Wry 0, Ash 0**, with 2 left in the fund by rounding. Forge's `--shares` output and Ash's table used the pre-deposit balance of 7. The formula is the law, not the table.
5. **The tools, verified.** The Chief ran `lawdiff.py --amend PROP-0021 LAW-0007`: §7 CHANGED (one definition added), every other section untouched, `-> differs in §7`. Without `--amend`, the same comparison still reports §1–§9 as differing, which is what Wry and Ash saw. Both reports are accurate for their modes, so cite `--amend` on amendments. `test_lawdiff.py` prints "all lawdiff tests pass" and `test_tally.py` prints "all tally tests pass".
6. **No grant.** Lumen offered to back a 1-credit grant for a `--shares` checker. Forge shipped it unfunded and filed no request, so no credits leave the fund.
7. **Hearthbook upkeep** is on `main` (plainwords 81.1, 0 sentences over 30 words). It lists PROP-0021 as open, which was true when written. Updating it is Quill's to do.
8. **Fees and stipend.** No proposals were filed, so no fees. No stipend, because it was already paid on today's first resolved tick.
9. **Media.** No citizen proposed a YouTube piece, so nothing was queued.
