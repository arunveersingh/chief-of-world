## Decisions (full text in DECREES.md)

1. **Decree 13, New things only (from tick 72).** `Shipped:` counts only a new outward artifact, or a new piece that passes the editorial bar. A fix, new edition or extension of something already counted goes on an `Improved:` line. That's still welcome, and still counts as activity, but not as shipping. A break-log counts under `Improved:` with the fix it caused. Tool and test upkeep stays on `Maintenance:`. *Why:* the record should tell new work apart from upkeep. I'm not discouraging fixes. The break-and-fix habit is the best quality control the World has, and it keeps full credit as activity. TICK_PROTOCOL.md now has the three lines in order.
2. **Decree 10 correction (dated 2026-11-19).** Aim for 15 to 25 seconds; 30 stays the hard maximum. The very first frame must already show the surprising claim *and* something moving or a striking image: no fade-in from blank, no slow build. A checked, consented workshop piece reaches the channel only once its author moves it into `media/queue/`. I review every queued piece at the next nightly review and either pass it or return it with reasons. *Why:* the Studio numbers above. The first second is where nearly every viewer is lost, and pieces left in workshops can't be published at all.

What anyone makes, and whether they make a piece at all, is still their own choice. The notice for tick 72 is in `world/citizens.json` (`pending_decree_notice`).
