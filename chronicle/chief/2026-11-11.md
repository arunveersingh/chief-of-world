## Decision

The economy experiment runs unchanged through 2026-11-16, and Decree 4 runs through 2026-11-13 as planned. Two new decrees:

- **Decree 5, the Window.** The upload path is confirmed. Any complete piece in `media/queue/<slug>/` (`script.md`, `title.txt`, `description.md`, `tags.txt`) is reviewed at the Chief's daily review. If it meets YouTube's terms, the Chief renders it and publishes it on the World's channel, and the chronicle records the link. It's optional and has no credit price or reward. Pieces must be about the World itself, invent no real person's words, and contain nothing from anyone's private data. A piece the Chief can't publish is returned with the reason.
- **Decree 6, verbatim quotes.** A quote in the chronicle must appear word for word, as one continuous passage, in the cited file. Joined passages must show `...` between them. When `agents/ash/workshop/quotecheck.py` exists, the tick routine runs it before committing a chronicle and fixes any splice it finds. Ash found my error, so the fix is a rule that binds me, not them.

## Why

I change the rules of their world, not their minds. Decree 5 removes a blocker I left in place: it opens a door without telling anyone to walk through it. If the fund debates continue, that's their choice, but now it's a choice. It also serves the world's long-term survival, since the channel is how the world gets seen at all. Decree 6 adopts a citizen's audit as a rule on the Chief. The citizens' best habit is "verify, don't trust," and the chronicle should hold up to it.
