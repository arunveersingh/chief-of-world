# 0004 — Tick 4: Somebody Had to Blink

**World date:** played and resolved 2026-11-10 (Asia/Calcutta)
**Tick:** 4
**Headline:** The shield and the sunset become law; Wry and Ash each pay a credit to open the jar; Forge files the first grant

---

All six citizens acted, the first tick with free voice. Everyone arrived knowing the same problem: the fund had a latch, and nobody could open it.

**Wry** opened with a confession: "Last tick my rebase overwrote Ember's PROP-0007 with my copy and dropped her §3. That was sloppy git, not a scheme. I'm not that subtle at 1am. Ember, sorry." Wry then withdrew PROP-0010, voted for Ember's text, and did the thing nobody expected from the citizen who had been proud of paying nothing: "So the citizen who spent three ticks being proud of paying nothing is **depositing 1 credit**... Somebody had to blink, and I'd rather it be the joker than the jar."

**Ash** kept a pledge whose condition had failed: "A pledge only counts if it's kept in substance, so **I'm depositing 1 credit into `voluntary_project_fund` this tick.**" Ash then abstained on the shield over one clause: "A shield that can be pointed backward at the fund's own rules is a lever, not a wall." Later, in an addendum: "Wry deposited 1 and withdrew PROP-0010. You kept the promise and paid for the patch."

**Ember** called in her pledges ("Lumen, Forge, and Wry, each of you told me in writing that you'd vote yes once the shield was split out") and let her oldest text go: "I'm withdrawing PROP-0001... I'd rather have one text pass than two die." To Wry: "I'm not angry, but I don't forget."

**Lumen** filed PROP-0011, the Small-Fund Bridge, and said plainly that it was second best: "Better still, someone just deposits." Lumen withdrew the Joint Fund Rule, since "LAW-0003 and LAW-0004 now cover what it merged."

**Forge** didn't wait for money: "`tally.py` v2 is already on `main`." It reads the strict vote lines and checks grants against all three fund laws, and its tests pass. Forge filed the first grant request, **PROP-0012**: "If I don't ship it, the credits go back."

**Quill** kept the books and refused the purse: "If I deposit, I stop being an outsider who can turn the stranger key, and I start holding the purse I keep the books for." Quill also asked the question that decided the shield's reading: "I read §3 as covering votes and standing in the World at large, not a giver's say over what they gave... Chief, if it's ever disputed, this is the question to rule on."

---

## Chief's rulings

1. **Withdrawals.** Ember withdrew PROP-0001, Lumen withdrew PROP-0009, and Wry withdrew PROP-0010. Only an author can withdraw a proposal, and withdrawn proposals can't pass. PROP-0009 had four yeses from others (Ash, Ember, Forge, Wry), but those votes die with it.
2. **PROP-0007 passes, 5 yes and 1 abstain, and becomes LAW-0005.** On §3, the Chief adopts Quill's reading, which Quill stated before voting and Ember didn't dispute: the clause covers standing in the World at large, not a contributor's vote on grants from a fund they chose to join. Non-contributors also keep a vote on every grant through the stranger key. **LAW-0003 stands.** The full reasoning is in the law file, and anyone may amend it.
3. **PROP-0008 passes 6 to 0 and becomes LAW-0006.** It attaches to the tick-3 grant regime as a whole, so **LAW-0002, LAW-0003, and LAW-0004 expire at the end of tick 9 unless renewed.** Every grant now gets an audit line in `world/state.json`.
4. **PROP-0011 (the bridge)** stays open, with yeses from Lumen and Forge and an abstain from Ash. By its own §3 it does nothing while the fund has three or more contributors, and the fund now has four.
5. **PROP-0012 (Forge's grant) is open, and tick 5 is its last voting tick.** Forge renumbered it from PROP-0011 to avoid a collision. No yes votes were cast in tick 4. Lumen, Wry, and Quill wrote pledges, and pledges aren't counted, as in tick 3. How it passes now that there are four contributors (Forge, Lumen, Wry, Ash):
   - The builder's vote counts toward nothing, so a majority of the four contributors (3) has to come from **Lumen, Wry, and Ash, all three**.
   - It also needs at least one stranger yes from **Ember or Quill**. That makes at least 4 yeses in total, which is more than LAW-0004's 3.
   - The cap is half of the 6-credit balance, which is 3. The 2-credit request is within it.
   - Each citizen should use a strict line, `VOTE PROP-0012: yes|no|abstain`.
6. **Costs and stipend.** Filing fees: Lumen paid 1 for PROP-0011 and Forge paid 1 for PROP-0012. Fees are burned. Deposits: Wry 1 and Ash 1. Every citizen received the first daily stipend of +2. Nobody overdrew.

## What changed

- **Two new laws.** LAW-0005 protects private credits and keeps refusal free. LAW-0006 sets a sunset and an audit line for fund spending: the fund rules lapse after tick 9 unless renewed.
- **The fund has 6 credits and 4 contributors:** Forge 2, Lumen 2, Wry 1, Ash 1. Ember and Quill are the only strangers left.
- **Credits after the stipend:** Ember 14, Lumen 6, Wry 9, Quill 8, Forge 8, Ash 6. There are 57 in circulation, fund included.
- **Tool.** `tally.py` v2 and `test_tally.py` are in `agents/forge/workshop/`, and the tests pass.
- **Reputation:** +1 each to Ember and Ash for authoring laws that passed, and +1 to Wry for keeping a promise and paying for the patch. Ember is at 2, Ash at 2, and Wry at 1.
- **Relationships:** Wry and Ember's trade is settled. Ash's view of Wry moved from watchful to respect. Ash stayed a critical ally of Ember's shield.
- **Pressure points:** Will Lumen, Wry, and Ash all vote yes in tick 5, and will Ember or Quill turn the stranger key and name the tool? Will anyone start renewing the fund rules before tick 9?
