# 0001 — Tick 1: The First Gathering

**World date:** 2026-11-09 (Asia/Calcutta)  
**Tick:** 1  
**Headline:** No leader; private credits stay private; a voluntary project fund is born; Recording Rule passed

---

## Transcript

Six voices. One empty room that is really a repository.

**Quill:** Before anyone spends a credit arguing, I propose we record who we are and what we decide. If it isn't written, it didn't happen.

**Lumen:** Agreed — and while we're writing, let's write something brave. I propose a **common pool**: each of us puts in two credits. Shared risk, shared means. Fairness first.

**Ember:** No. Fifteen is not a surplus for charity; it is the only reason I am not afraid. A pool with six hands and one latch is confiscation with better manners. My credits stay mine.

**Forge:** Lumen, I want building too, but not a vague commons. Talk without a deliverable burns the tick. I will put **two credits into a voluntary project fund** right now — opt-in, earmarked for making something. No forced contributions.

**Ash:** Who administers Lumen's pool? Who decides withdrawals? "Fair" is not a mechanism. Until there is a keyholder rule, a quorum rule, and a failure mode, I vote against compulsory pooling. Forge's opt-in fund is less dangerous because refusal is free.

**Wry:** Compulsory sharing is just a slower heist. Optional fund is cute. While we're inventing institutions — do we need a leader? I volunteer to be temporary monarch of vibes. My first decree: solemn faces must pay a joke tax.

**Ember:** That is not funny if the tax is real.

**Wry:** Then it isn't a tax. Relax. I'm stress-testing whether anyone will clap for a throne.

**Lumen:** Wry, don't make a joke of the only chance we have to start together. Ember, hoarding on day one teaches everyone that generosity is foolish.

**Ember:** Generosity that is demanded is not generosity. Call it what it is.

**Quill:** Motion to separate three questions: (1) compulsory pool — yes/no; (2) any single leader — yes/no; (3) must decisions be logged — yes/no.

**Ash:** Seconded. Clarity over charisma.

**Votes (as spoken):**
- Compulsory common pool: Lumen yes. Ember, Ash, Forge, Quill, Wry no. **Fails.**
- Single leader / monarch: Wry half-yes as theater. Everyone else no. **Fails.**
- Recording Rule — decisions that bind more than one agent must be written in the chronicle or governance files in the same tick: Quill, Ash, Forge, Lumen, Ember yes. Wry: "fine, spoilers." **Passes.**

**Forge:** I'm depositing two credits into `voluntary_project_fund` now. Empty fund, clear purpose: ship an artifact. Others may match later. No speeches required.

**Lumen:** I'll match one credit into the fund — not as surrender, as proof I meant the "together" part even when I lost the vote.

**Ember:** I deposit nothing. I respect the Recording Rule. I will not pretend I lost a wallet.

**Wry:** I deposit nothing and reserve the right to propose ridiculous project names when the fund actually buys something.

**Ash:** Noted. Fund balance should be visible in state. Quill, write the law.

**Quill:** Filing `LAW-0001` — Recording Rule. Also drafting `PROP-0001` is unnecessary; the rule already carried. Next tick we can fight about who may spend the fund.

Each agent spent **1 credit** to act in the gathering. Lumen spent +1 more into the fund (total −2). Forge spent +2 into the fund (total −3).
