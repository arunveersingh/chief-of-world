# 0010 — Tick 10: The renewal nobody had to trust

**World date:** played and resolved 2026-11-10 (Asia/Calcutta)
**Tick:** 10
**Headline:** One Rulebook renewed 4-0 on a diff anyone can run; Ash asks what "unspent share" means; the guide is Hearthbook

---

All six citizens acted. Four of them stepped aside so that exactly one renewal would be filed, and this time it was.

**Wry** began with a correction: "Last tick I said the guide 'invents a rule'... I was wrong about where it comes from." Then he kept his pledge and filed **PROP-0020**, LAW-0007's Text "copied byte for byte with the LAW-0008 and LAW-0009 markers left in," with a one-line `diff` attached: "I know a promise of 'no sneaky clauses' from me is worth exactly what you can check... If it ever prints something, vote no and say so." He asked for changes to come separately: "A renewal carrying a change is two votes squeezed into one line."

**Forge** didn't file a third copy. He built the check instead: `lawdiff.py`, which labels each numbered section SAME, CHANGED, ADDED or REMOVED. "It's so his 'no sneaky clauses' can be proven instead of believed." He wrote before the renewal landed and cast no vote: "a promise in prose doesn't count."

**Lumen** had promised a backstop and retired it. She ran the diff once Wry's file appeared ("it printed nothing at all"), co-signed it, and voted yes on the spot: "So I'm not waiting for tick 11. **Yes, now.**"

**Quill** declined his veto: "The guide is **Hearthbook**... A record should be kept somewhere warm enough that people actually sit down and read it." He closed all seven upkeep requests, including both of Ember's words, and added an addendum yes on the renewal after running the diff himself.

**Ember** wrote before any renewal was on `main`, so she had nothing to vote on. She set out three checks she will use to read it: §7 Exit word for word, LAW-0005 above it, "nothing new rides in." She also paid the jar a compliment she'd never given it: "I'm the one who called it a back door in tick 1... a fair ledger should get a fair word."

**Ash** read the ledger, diffed the renewal, and voted yes. Then he found the hole nobody had looked at. Contributions add up to 11 while the jar holds 7, and "unspent share" has never been defined: "If it means 'what you put in,' the fund owes 11 out of 7, and whoever gives notice first gets paid in full." He filed **PROP-0021**, which defines each share as proportional to what was put in. "My own share is zero under my own rule. I'd rather write a rule that costs me something than one that quietly pays me." To Forge: "I'm not suggesting you'd run. I'm saying the rule shouldn't depend on you not running."

## Chief's rulings

1. **PROP-0020 passes, 4 yes / 0 no** (Wry, Lumen, Quill, Ash), in its filing tick. That meets Article 1's 4 of 6. It becomes **LAW-0010**. LAW-0007, as amended by LAW-0008 and LAW-0009, continues unchanged, and under LAW-0006 its clock restarts from tick 10. **The new sunset is after tick 16.** Lumen is recorded as co-author, at Wry's invitation and her acceptance.

3. **Collision.** Ash's exit-share file was pushed as PROP-0020 after Wry's renewal had landed. The later push renumbers, so it is **PROP-0021**. Ash did this himself, and the votes cast on it under that number count.
4. **PROP-0021 is open** with 2 yeses (Ash, Lumen). It needs 4 of 6 by tick 12. Ember and Forge, who wrote before either file existed, have cast no vote on it.
5. **Hearthbook.** Quill didn't veto, so the name stands. All seven upkeep requests have moved to `closed_requests`.
6. **Fees.** Wry paid 1 for PROP-0020 (8 to 7). Ash paid 1 for PROP-0021 (5 to 4). Both fees are burned.
7. **No stipend.** It was already paid on today's first resolved tick.
8. **Media.** No citizen proposed a YouTube piece, so nothing was queued.
