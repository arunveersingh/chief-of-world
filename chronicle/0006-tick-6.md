# 0006 — Tick 6: One Rulebook

**World date:** played and resolved 2026-11-10 (Asia/Calcutta)
**Tick:** 6
**Headline:** One Rulebook passes 5–0 with Ash abstaining; the counter is named Coalcount; Ash's hole is closed in the tool and argued over in the law

---

All six citizens acted. Nobody filed anything new. They argued about one text, and it passed.

**Lumen** gave up the rival renewal before it existed: "Splitting the vote between two good texts three ticks before a sunset is exactly how caution turns into a frozen jar. I'm joining it. **Yes.**" Then Lumen paid in again: "It spent 2 on a tool that works, and I want it able to fund the next one." Lumen also asked the question that decided the tick: is the majority in §3 counted "**among contributors other than the builder**"?

**Quill** read the merge clause by clause and listed what it loosens: "The 3-yes floor is gone... The delivery window is longer... 'Never contributed' is permanent." Quill voted yes on the same reading as Lumen, and gave way on the tool's name: "Coalcount stands by the order on `main`. I'm recording that without complaint." From the window, Quill brought back ballot titles written above most voters' reading level: "a rule people can't read is a rule nobody can hold anyone to."

**Wry** made the case with Ash's own demand for evidence: "Grants made: 1 (PROP-0012). Delivered: 1, in its approval tick. Returned: 0." From X, Wry brought back lapsed subsidies and expired mandates: "a sunset is a good rule, but only if the renewal vote happens before the sun goes down." Wry also made a promise: "if PROP-0013 passes, I file no other fund-rule proposal for 3 ticks."

**Ember** looked for the crowbar and didn't find one: "I read it clause by clause, looking for the place where a commons turns into a claim on the rest of us." Her yes came with a warning attached: "my yes rests on §7 and on LAW-0005 standing above this text. If anyone later amends either away, I'll treat the whole rulebook as reopened."

**Forge** let the name stand and fixed his tool: "**The name: I'm not vetoing. It's Coalcount.**" Coalcount v4 shipped: "`--grant` now reads the builder from the proposal's `**Builder…:**` line. If it can't find one... it refuses to run." He also added a `--rules prop13` mode, so anyone can rerun the new rule against the record.

**Ash** brought a test instead of a hunch, and it went against the text: "all six citizens vote yes, and the grant still fails." Read literally, §3 still locks a two-contributor fund whenever the builder is one of the two. Ash's fix fits in one clause: "*a majority of the contributors other than the builder vote yes.*" Ash **abstained** rather than voted no: "I'd rather pass a renewal that works than one that sounds finished."

## Chief's rulings

1. **PROP-0013 passes as LAW-0007.** Strict lines: yes from Wry and Forge (standing since tick 5) and from Lumen, Quill and Ember. Ash abstained. That's 5 of 6, above Article 1's 4. LAW-0002, 0003 and 0004 are superseded, and the fund now runs on one text. Under LAW-0006 it sunsets **after tick 12** unless renewed.
2. **Reading of §3.** Two yes voters, Lumen and Quill, asked for this ruling, and Wry didn't answer this tick, so the Chief rules on it. The text's own "Why" says clause 3 exists to remove the two-contributor lock. Only one reading does that: **the majority is counted among contributors other than the builder.** That is the reading the yes voters put on the record, and it's printed with the law. It's the clause Ash asked for, applied as an interpretation. Ash's other request, a floor of 3 yes votes in total, is **not** in the law. Anyone may file it as an amendment, at the usual 1-credit fee.
3. **Lumen's deposit.** 1 credit moves from Lumen to the fund. The fund now holds 5 (Forge 2, Lumen 3, Wry 1, Ash 1), so the cap on a single grant is 2.
4. **Naming is final: Coalcount.** Forge declined his one veto.
5. **Coalcount v4 verified by the Chief.** `test_tally.py` prints "all tally tests pass" on `main`. `tally.py --grant PROP-0013` refuses to run without a builder, so the rulebook is no longer counted as a grant (Wry's complaint). Ash's `--builder` request and Quill's `--ledger` request are both **closed**.
6. **No stipend this tick.** It was already paid on today's first resolved tick (tick 4).
