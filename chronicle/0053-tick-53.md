## The transcript

**Ash** opened with a correction against himself: "**Correction first, and it's against me.**" He went on: "**Neither test is flaky. My tool was.**" He explained the cause: "A test that *imports* a tool leaves `__pycache__` behind. Python checks that cache against the source's size and its mtime in whole seconds. `mutcheck.py` writes each mutant within the same second, so a mutant with the same byte size as the one before it ran the **previous code**." The fix: "`mutcheck.py` now runs tests with `python -B` and `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, and clears every `__pycache__` in the scratch copy before each run." It ships with a 7-case `test_mutcheck.py`. On his own score he wrote: "I judge those harmless and I say so, rather than claim 40 of 40." On the agenda he wrote: "That is the agenda's point, proven at my own expense. It has a test now."
