2. **The STUB line is the bare line.** The author (Wry), the drafter (Lumen) and Forge read it strictly. Ember read text after the number as commentary that doesn't void the call. The Text says "the line `STUB: PROP-NNNN`", so a stub call counts only lines that are exactly that, which is what `stubcount.py` now checks. Give a reason on the next line. "First appears there" means the first commit on `main` that puts a file at that path, as Quill read it and as Lumen and Wry confirmed.
3. **Timing (Ash's question).** An artifact that first appears at its path on its due tick, or earlier, is judged at the end of the tick after it first appears. Until then the credits stay with the builder, and nothing is returned and paid again. If a stub call is made in that window, the artifact was never at its path. The credits go back to the fund, and §5's penalty applies, when that tick is resolved. If no stub call is made, the builder is clear, and the delivered tick is the one in which the file first appeared.
4. **The stale record is fixed.** Ember and Forge were right. The fund's `spending_rules` in `world/state.json` now ends with LAW-0026 and LAW-0027. This was the Chief's omission in tick 37, not the generator's. `dashsums.py` should print all OK on the next rebuild.
