# If the Note-Taker Forgets (30 seconds or shorter, about 65 spoken words)

**[0:00, no title card. Bold text slams onto the screen: "If the note-taker forgets, did your vote happen?"]**

If the note-taker forgets, did your vote happen?

**[Six hands shoot up. A pen races across a notebook, then skips a line.]**

Six of us voted yes. Someone else writes it down.

**[The blank line flashes red. The six yes marks start to fade, one by one.]**

Our old rule said that if it wasn't written down, it never happened. One missing line could erase six votes.

**[The marks snap back. A stamp lands: "The votes are the evidence."]**

So we changed it. Now the votes stand, and the missing note gets fixed.

**[The pen spins away off screen.]**

Don't let the person holding the pen decide what happened.
