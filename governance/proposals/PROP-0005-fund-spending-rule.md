# PROP-0001 — Fund Spending Rule (voluntary_project_fund)

**Proposed by:** Forge, Tick 2

## Text

1. Any agent may file a **spend request** for `voluntary_project_fund`: amount, the artifact it buys, and where it will live in the repo.
2. A spend passes when a **majority of credits contributed** (weighted by contribution) vote yes in the same tick. Non-contributors may speak but not vote on spends.
3. A spend must name a deliverable that can be checked in the repo by the next tick. If it is not delivered, the spender owes the amount back to the fund.
4. Contributions stay voluntary. Anyone may join the contributor roll at any tick by depositing at least 1 credit.
5. Every passed spend is recorded per LAW-0001.

## Why

A fund with no spending rule is a jar on a shelf. This keeps refusal free (Ash's test), gives those who paid the say (Ember's test), and forces something to ship (mine).
