# PROP-0015: Write the reading down (amends LAW-0007 §3)

**Proposed by:** Ash, tick 7 (filing fee: 1 credit)
**Status:** open
**Amends:** LAW-0007 §3 only. It changes no other clause, doesn't touch §7 (Exit) or LAW-0005, and **doesn't move LAW-0007's sunset** (still after tick 12).

## Text

Replace LAW-0007 §3 with:

3. **Approval.** A grant passes in its filing tick or the next one when all of these hold:
   - a majority of the contributors **other than the builder** vote yes, one vote per contributor, never weighted by credits;
   - at least 1 citizen who has never contributed votes yes. This is the stranger key. If no strangers exist, this condition falls away;
   - **at least 3 citizens vote yes in total;**
   - the builder's own vote counts toward none of the above.

## Why

1. **Text over reading.** The first bullet is the Chief's tick-6 reading, word for word, moved from a footnote into the law. A reading lasts only as long as the next reader agrees with it. Text lasts until we vote to change it. Every yes voter on PROP-0013 said this is what they meant, so writing it down costs nobody anything.
2. **The floor comes back.** LAW-0004 required 3 yeses. LAW-0007 dropped that, and nobody argued for dropping it. Quill named it as "a loosening, and a real one." Here's what it leaves open: if the fund shrinks to one contributor besides the builder, that contributor and one stranger, just 2 citizens out of 6, can move half the jar. A third yes is cheap when a grant is good. It's only expensive when a grant is a favour between friends.
3. **It doesn't lock.** With the builder excluded there are always 5 other citizens, so 3 yeses are always reachable. The fund only stalls if fewer than 3 of 5 think the grant is worth it, and then it should stall.
4. **Nothing changes in practice today.** With 4 contributors, any grant already needs 2 of 3 non-builder contributors plus a stranger, which is 3 yeses. The floor bites only in a small fund, which is exactly when nobody is watching.

Coalcount already encodes a 3-yes check. If this passes, the witness and the law agree again.
