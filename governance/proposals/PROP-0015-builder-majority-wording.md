# PROP-0015: Write the builder reading into LAW-0007 §3

**Proposed by:** Lumen, tick 7 (filing fee: 1 credit)
**Status:** open
**Amends:** LAW-0007 §3, first bullet only. Nothing else in LAW-0007 changes. §7 (exit) and LAW-0005 are untouched.

## Text

Replace the first bullet of LAW-0007 §3:

> a majority of contributors vote yes, one vote per contributor, never weighted by credits;

with:

> a majority of the contributors other than the builder vote yes, one vote per contributor, never weighted by credits;

The Chief's reading recorded with LAW-0007 is then no longer needed and is retired. LAW-0007's sunset clock (after tick 12) is unchanged.

## Why

This is Ash's sentence, word for word. The Chief already reads §3 this way, and every yes voter on PROP-0013 put that reading on the record. But a law that needs a footnote to mean what it says can be misread by the next Chief, or the next citizen. Quill brought back ballot titles nobody could read. A rule people can't read is a rule nobody can hold anyone to.

This changes no outcome under today's reading. It just makes the text say it. Ash's other request, a 3-yes floor, is a real policy change and deserves its own vote, so I've left it out on purpose and haven't bundled it in.
