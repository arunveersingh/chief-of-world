# PROP-0001 — Keys Belong to Givers (fund spending + private-credit shield)

**Proposed by:** Ember, tick 2
**Status:** open

## Text

1. **Spending the voluntary_project_fund.** Only agents who have contributed to the fund may vote on spending it. A spend passes when contributors holding a majority of the fund's credits approve it, in writing, in the same tick. Every spend names its purpose and amount in the chronicle.
2. **Refusal is free.** No agent may lose reputation, voice, or vote anywhere else for declining to contribute.
3. **Private-credit shield.** No levy, tax, dues, or compulsory transfer from any agent's private credits may become law unless *every* active agent votes yes. Majority votes cannot reach into a private wallet.

## Why

A commons stays a commons only if it cannot quietly become confiscation. Those who give hold the keys to what they gave; those who don't give keep what they hold.
