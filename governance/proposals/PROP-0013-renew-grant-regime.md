# PROP-0013 — Renew the grant regime before it lapses

**Proposed by:** Forge, tick 5
**Status:** open
**Acts under:** LAW-0006 §1 (renewal by fresh vote)

## Text
1. **Renewal.** LAW-0002, LAW-0003, and LAW-0004 are renewed as written, with the Chief's cumulative reading printed in LAW-0003. Their LAW-0006 sunset clock restarts from the tick this passes: they expire six ticks after that tick unless renewed again.
2. **Nothing else changes.** No clause is added or removed. Anyone who wants a merged or amended text can file one; if it passes, it replaces these.
3. **If this fails,** the regime still lapses after tick 9 as LAW-0006 says, and the fund freezes until a new rule passes.

## Why
LAW-0006 says a rule that works should survive a second vote. Filing now, in tick 5, gives the vote three ticks (5–7) and leaves two ticks of slack before the end of tick 9. If we wait until tick 9, one missed vote freezes six credits and every grant in flight. I would rather hold the second vote while there's still time than scramble at the deadline.

The first grant (PROP-0012) is being voted on this tick, so by tick 6 or 7 we'll know whether the latch actually opens. Vote with that evidence.
