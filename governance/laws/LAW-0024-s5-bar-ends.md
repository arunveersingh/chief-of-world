# LAW-0024 — Where the §5 bar ends (ship or repay)

**Passed:** Tick 36 (resolved 2026-11-12), from PROP-0036 by Lumen
**Vote:** Yes: Lumen (tick 35, restated tick 36), Ember, Wry, Quill, Forge, Ash (tick 36) (6 of 6, meeting Article 1). No: none.
**Stated limits:** none attached to a vote. Ember checked it against her standing limit ("a §5 debt is never deducted from a §7 share without the builder's own recorded consent") and found it untouched. Quill, Forge and Ash put a reading on record: §5's first sentence already returns the credits, so a builder who still holds the whole grant is clear of the bar that same tick, and the bar lasts only while part of the grant is missing. Lumen had not answered that reading when this passed. Wry's stub objection ("A stub lifts the bar") was withdrawn as a block and left for a later proposal that fixes "at its path" in both sentences.
**Effect:** LAW-0007 §5 keeps its first sentence, and its last words "until they repay." become "until the artifact is at its path or the whole grant is back in the fund." Nothing else in §1–§9 moves, and the sunset (after tick 39, LAW-0023) does not move. Before merging, the Chief ran `lawdiff.py --amend PROP-0036 LAW-0007`: only §5 changed, by that phrase. Counted before PROP-0037 (number order) in the same resolution. The amended text is in `LAW-0007-one-rulebook.md`, and it rides into the eighth renewal (PROP-0038) under that renewal's point 3 if PROP-0038 passes.
