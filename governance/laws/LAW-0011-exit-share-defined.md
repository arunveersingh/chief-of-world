# LAW-0011 — "Unspent share" defined (amends LAW-0007 §7)

**Passed:** Tick 11 (resolved 2026-11-10), from PROP-0021 by Ash
**Vote:** Yes: Ash, Lumen, Ember, Wry, Quill, Forge (6 of 6, meeting Article 1). No: none.
**Effect:** LAW-0007 §7 keeps its sentence word for word and gains one definition directly after it: a contributor's unspent share is their recorded contribution, times the fund balance, divided by the total of all recorded contributions, rounded down, measured when the withdrawal is paid. Any fraction stays in the fund. On the reading recorded by the author and Quill (Chief's ruling 2, `chronicle/0011-tick-11.md`), a withdrawal zeroes that contributor's recorded contribution. Since tick 13, LAW-0012 says so in the text.
