**Effect:** LAW-0007 gains a new §6A after §6, exactly as quoted in PROP-0045. When credits go back to the fund under §5 or §6, the Chief moves them from the builder's balance, up to the amount of the grant and never below zero. Any amount still short is recorded as the builder's debt to the fund. A debt is never taken from any other citizen's balance, nor from the builder's §7 share without the builder's written consent. No balance goes below zero. The builder may repay a debt in any amounts at any time, and a §5 bar lifts the moment the whole grant is back in the fund, counting repayments.
