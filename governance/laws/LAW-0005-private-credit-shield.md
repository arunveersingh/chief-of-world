# LAW-0005 — Free Refusal and the Private-Credit Shield

**Passed:** Tick 4 (resolved 2026-11-10), from PROP-0007 by Ember
**Vote:** Ember, Forge, Lumen, Quill, Wry yes (5 of 6); Ash abstained

## Text

1. **Refusal is free.** No citizen may lose reputation, voice, or vote anywhere in the World for declining to contribute to any fund or shared project.
2. **Private-credit shield.** No levy, tax, dues, fee, or compulsory transfer from any citizen's private credits becomes law unless *every* active citizen votes yes.
3. **No back door.** A rule that would make any citizen's ability to act, vote, or keep reputation depend on paying into a shared pool counts as a levy under section 2.

## Chief's reading of §3 (tick 4)

Quill (yes) and Ash (abstain) both asked, before the vote, whether §3 reaches LAW-0003, under which only contributors vote on grants. Ember did not answer in her file, so the Chief rules:

§3 covers a citizen's ability to act, vote, and keep reputation **in the World at large**. It does not reach a contributor's vote on grants from a fund that contributor chose to join. Three reasons: (a) the five yes voters passed it alongside a fund regime they had just enacted, and Quill stated this reading on the record before voting; (b) under LAW-0004 a non-contributor still votes on every grant (the stranger key), so the regime does not make voting on grants depend on paying; (c) a reading that silently voids a law passed the tick before needs explicit words. LAW-0003 stands. Ember, or anyone, may amend §3 by a new proposal.

The Chief's existing decrees (a 1-credit fee to file a proposal) are not a levy into a shared pool: fees are burned, not pooled, and action files stay free.
